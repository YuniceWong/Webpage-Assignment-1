SIMON PORTER JACQUEMUS

Not just making clothes,

But telling a story.

Simon Porte Jacquemus is a self-taught French designer from the South of France who founded Jacquemus in 2009 at just 19 years old. After briefly studying at fashion school in Paris, he left to start his own label following the passing of his mother, who remains a major inspiration behind his work. His designs are influenced by childhood memories, family, and Mediterranean culture, and are known for their simplicity, bold shapes, and poetic storytelling.

OUR BRAND STORY

Jacquemus was founded in 2009 by Simon Porte Jacquemus at just 19 years old. Inspired by his childhood in the South of France and the memory of his late mother, whose maiden name he used for the brand.

Jacquemus began as a deeply personal project rooted in emotion and storytelling. Without formal training, Simon built the label with a fresh, unconventional approach.

The brand is known for bold designs, exaggerated proportions, and warm Mediterranean influences.
